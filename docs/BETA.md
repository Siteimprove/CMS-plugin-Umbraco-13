# Historical customer beta

The beta series ended with **1.0.0-beta.8**. Current installation and release
information is in [package information](DISTRIBUTION.md),
[installation](INSTALLATION.md) and the [1.0.0 release notes](releases/1.0.0.md).

Previously distributed beta ZIPs retain their own guides, checksums and provenance.
Do not edit or replace those immutable assets. The compatibility command
`python3 scripts/verify-beta-bundle.py <bundle.zip> --require-ci` continues to
verify their original contents.
